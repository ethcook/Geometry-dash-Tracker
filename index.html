🌱 For Beginners

Start your Geometry Dash journey one step at a time.

You can start off by beating main levels like Stereo Madness, Back on Track, and more. Beat some of them and start playing some online levels and demons.

1

Build your foundation

You can start off with beating main levels like Stereo Madness, Back on Track, and more. Beat some of them to learn control, rhythm, and timing.

2

Take on online levels and demons

After you feel more confident, start playing some online levels and demons. This helps you learn different layouts and challenge types.

3

Move into medium demons

Once you beat 1-3 demons, you can head to medium demons like B and Skeletal Shenanigans, plus other medium demon routes. Those are different demons, so you can get comfortable with more varied mechanics.

4

Push toward harder demon content

After that, you can beat insane demons like Acropolis and then take on extreme demons like Cataclysm or Acu. Then go to hard demons like Nine Circles. Cause being good at wave is crucial, and training with Crazy and Future Funk will help you improve your wave control and reaction speed.

How to Use Geometry Dash Tracker

What this site is for

This tracker helps you improve at Geometry Dash by keeping a record of your goals, demons, practice sessions, and weaknesses.

How to use it

  • Add goals to stay focused on what you want to beat next.
  • Log demons you beat with difficulty, attempts, and notes.
  • Track practice sessions to see how much time you spend improving.
  • Mark your weak mechanics to focus on the sections you need to practice.
  • Visit the dashboard for quick stats and the timeline for progress over time.

About the website

This tool is designed for Geometry Dash players who want a simple, local tracker for practice, demons, and improvement goals. Your data stays in your browser.
